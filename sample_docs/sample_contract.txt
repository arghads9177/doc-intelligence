SERVICE AGREEMENT

This Service Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into on March 1, 2024 ("Effective Date"),
by and between Acme Corporation ("Service Provider") and XYZ Limited ("Client").

1. SERVICE DESCRIPTION
Service Provider agrees to provide professional consulting services including:
- Business strategy development
- Process optimization
- Technology implementation support
- Training and documentation

2. TERM
This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and continue for a period of 
24 months, ending on February 28, 2026 ("Term"), unless earlier terminated 
in accordance with the provisions herein.

3. FEES AND PAYMENT
3.1 The Client shall pay Service Provider a monthly fee of $5,000 USD, due on 
    the first business day of each month.
3.2 All payments shall be made in US Dollars via wire transfer to the account 
    specified by Service Provider.
3.3 Late payments shall accrue interest at 1.5% per month.

4. OBLIGATIONS OF SERVICE PROVIDER
4.1 Maintain professional standards in all deliverables
4.2 Respond to client inquiries within 48 business hours
4.3 Provide monthly progress reports
4.4 Maintain confidentiality of client information

5. OBLIGATIONS OF CLIENT
5.1 Provide timely access to necessary personnel and systems
5.2 Provide complete and accurate information required for services
5.3 Pay all invoices within 30 days of receipt

6. CONFIDENTIALITY
Both parties agree to maintain confidentiality of any proprietary information 
disclosed during the term of this Agreement and for 3 years thereafter.

7. TERMINATION
7.1 Either party may terminate this Agreement with 60 days written notice.
7.2 Upon termination, Client shall pay for all services rendered through the 
    termination date.
7.3 Service Provider shall return all Client materials within 30 days of termination.

8. LIABILITY LIMITATION
Neither party shall be liable for indirect, incidental, or consequential damages, 
regardless of the cause of action.

9. DISPUTE RESOLUTION
Any disputes arising under this Agreement shall be governed by the laws of 
the State of New York and resolved through binding arbitration.

10. ENTIRE AGREEMENT
This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties and supersedes 
all prior negotiations and agreements.
